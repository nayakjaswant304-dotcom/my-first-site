Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Multi-Topic Study Hub ("we", "the site") collects, how it is used, and the third-party services involved. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account creation or login. We collect information only in the following cases:

  • Contact Form: If you use the Contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide. This is sent via Web3Forms (a third-party form processing service) directly to our inbox.
  • Theme Preference: Your light/dark mode choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not collect quiz scores, study activity, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the Contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Web3Forms: Processes Contact form submissions. See Web3Forms' Privacy Policy for details.
  • Monetag: Displays advertisements on this site and may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. Monetag's practices are governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: Used for typography; this may involve a request to Google's font servers when the page loads.

These third parties may independently collect data (such as IP address or browser information) as part of their standard operation. We do not control their data practices.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This site itself does not set tracking cookies. Our advertising partner (Monetag) may use cookies or local identifiers to serve and measure ads. You can clear your browser's local storage or cookies at any time to remove any saved preferences or ad-related data.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for general audiences and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this site. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may use the site without submitting any personal information by simply not using the Contact form. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset your saved theme preference.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the Contact form on the home page.
